EMAE 285 · Lab 1 / Instrumentation · Automation

LVDT displacement calibration

Calibrated a linear variable differential transformer using known displacements and voltage output. Compared data-acquisition and multimeter readings, evaluated repeatability, linear operating range and departures from linearity.

Measured / considered

Known displacement, transducer voltage, instrument-to-instrument consistency.

Analysis

Calibration plot; measurement error; sensitivity and usable linear range.

Why it matters: In automated inspection, a sensor is only useful if its output is calibrated and its uncertainty understood.

EMAE 285 · Lab 2 / Mechanical testing · FEA

Pressure vessel stress & strain

Pressurized a thin-walled aluminum cylinder and investigated hoop and longitudinal stress. Applied thin-wall assumptions and considered strain relationships and the influence of Poisson’s ratio.

Measured / considered

Internal pressure, vessel dimensions, strain response.

Analysis

Measured-versus-theoretical strain/stress; assumptions and error discussion.

Why it matters: Directly relevant to pressure systems, energy equipment and validation of structural models.

EMAE 285 · Lab 3 / Thermal systems · Energy

Heated cylinder convection

Measured electrical heating input, cylinder and ambient temperatures, and airflow speed to estimate convective heat transfer. Compared Reynolds/Nusselt behavior with an empirical crossflow correlation.

Measured / considered

Voltage, current, surface temperature, ambient temperature, airspeed.

Analysis

Heat-transfer coefficient versus airflow; Nu–Re comparison; uncertainty.

Why it matters: Evidence of thermal instrumentation and energy-balance reasoning relevant to cooling and equipment engineering.

Friction across contact materials

Used an inclined plane to estimate static and kinetic friction for contact materials including felt and cork. Investigated onset of sliding and motion at different incline angles.

Measured / considered

Incline angle, transit time, material, test repetition.

Analysis

Friction coefficients by material and test condition.

Why it matters: Useful for fixture design, material handling, conveyors and motion systems.

Static versus dynamic spring stiffness

Tested three springs using static load–deflection and oscillation-based approaches. Examined how damping and natural frequency affect estimates of spring stiffness.

Measured / considered

Analysis

Static and dynamic stiffness estimates; method comparison.

Why it matters: Relevant to vibration isolation, machinery and component verification.

NACA 2415 airfoil wind tunnel

Measured surface pressure distributions on a NACA 2415 airfoil at 20 m/s over angles of attack from −10° to +20°. Investigated lift trends, stall and flow separation.

Measured / considered

Pressure taps, airspeed, angle of attack.

Analysis

Pressure coefficient distributions; lift trends; stall discussion.

Optical turbidity sensing

Collected optical-sensor voltages for turbidity standards, fitted a nonlinear calibration equation and developed an algorithm for repeated sampling and a stop condition. One report lists a 1.55% comparison error for a 250 NTU check.

Analysis

EMAE 285 · independently proposed experiment / Initiative · Vibration · Reliability

Resonance & tuned mass damper concept

Designed a one-degree-of-freedom flexible structure with a 100 g primary mass and a proposed 15 g secondary tuned mass damper. Planned a frequency sweep to compare vibration response before and after adding the damper.

Measured / considered

Proposed mass, stiffness, damping, forcing frequency.

Analysis

Predicted resonance curves; planned comparison (not measured results).

Why it matters: Demonstrates experimental initiative and the ability to turn vibration theory into a test plan.
